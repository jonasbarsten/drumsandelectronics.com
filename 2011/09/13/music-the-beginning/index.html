Music – The Beginning

This is a recording from a solo concert I did early June 2011.

The audio is just a stereo signal from my sound card, there are some audio drop-outs and I apologize for that.

In general I feel the tendency of looping is to present and that it makes the music less dynamic. I’m also growing tired of the first and last “theme”, those of you into experimental electronica might recognize it as Alva Noto’s “Garment”.

Feel free to comment.
